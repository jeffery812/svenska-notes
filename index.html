Numbers,
in Swedish.

02

A few patterns go a long way.

One number, two forms.

Use en with common-gender nouns and ett with neuter nouns. When counting on its own, use ett. Compounds ending in one also agree with the noun.

en bok · ett husone book · one house
tjugoen böcker · tjugoett hustwenty-one books · twenty-one houses

Tens first. Units next.

tjugo + tre = tjugotre

For 21–99, join the tens and units into one word. There is no “and” between them. Keep the standard written endings in tjugo, trettio, fyrtio.

fyrtiosju · nittioåttaforty-seven · ninety-eight

Change the last part.

tjugo + första = tjugoförsta

In a compound ordinal, only the last number becomes ordinal. Learn the irregular early forms separately; do not just add -de to every cardinal.

tjugofjärde · trettioandratwenty-fourth · thirty-second

A colon tells the order.

Use :a when the number ends in 1 or 2, except endings 11 and 12. Use :e for the rest. These are Swedish endings, rather than English “st”, “nd”, or “th”.

1:a · 2:a · 3:e · 21:a · 22:aBut: 11:e · 12:e · 111:e · 112:e

Hundreds & thousands.

Hundra and tusen mean one hundred and one thousand; ett hundra and ett tusen are also possible. Combine the parts without an English-style “and”.

tvåhundratrettiofyra234 · two hundred and thirty-four
tvåtusen · tretusenfemhundra2,000 · 3,500

Watch the big numbers.

Miljon and miljard behave as nouns: keep them separate and use a plural for two or more. Swedish miljard is “billion” in modern English.

en miljon · två miljonerone million · two million
en miljard · två miljarderone billion · two billion
Easy to mix up

Sjutton = 17; sjuttio = 70. Likewise, tretton / trettio = 13 / 30 and fjorton / fyrtio = 14 / 40. Pay attention to -ton versus -tio. These are spelling cues, not a pronunciation guide.

03

Put the numbers to work.

At a café · quantity

Två kaffe, tack.

Two coffees, please.
Use a cardinal when ordering a quantity.

In a building · position

Jag bor på tredje våningen.

I live on the third floor.
An ordinal identifies the floor in order.

On the calendar · date

Den tjugoförsta juni.

The twenty-first of June.
Write den 21 juni; read the day as an ordinal. Month names use lowercase.

Grammar note: den tredje boken uses den + ordinal + definite noun. In min första bok (“my first book”), the noun stays indefinite after the possessive.
